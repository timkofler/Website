Services

Everything your operation needs. From one partner.

From continuing airworthiness to parts and people: we support you where you need it, for a single task or as a long-term partner.

01

CAMO Technical Support & Engineering

Support for continuing airworthiness: maintenance programmes, AD and SB evaluation, reliability and technical records.

  • Maintenance programme development and revisions
  • AD and SB assessment and tracking
  • Reliability monitoring
  • Technical records and lease transitions
02

Flight Operations Engineering

Engineering support for your flight operations, from performance and weight and balance to operational procedures.

  • Aircraft performance and take-off analysis
  • Weight and balance
  • Operational procedures and EFB support
  • Route and airport assessments
03

Compliance Monitoring & Safety Management

Audits, compliance monitoring and SMS support that keep your organisation safe and ready for the next inspection.

  • Internal audits and compliance monitoring
  • SMS set-up and support
  • Preparation for authority audits
  • Follow-up of findings and corrective actions
04

Aviation Documentation & Publication

Manuals, procedures and technical publications, written, revised and kept up to date with current requirements.

  • CAME, MOE and operations manuals
  • Procedures and work instructions
  • Revisions to current regulations
  • Technical publications
05

Aircraft Materials & Logistics

Sourcing of parts and materials, from routine orders to AOG situations. Fast, traceable and with the right paperwork.

  • Worldwide parts sourcing
  • AOG support
  • Repairs and exchange units
  • Certificates such as EASA Form 1 and FAA 8130-3
06

Aviation Workforce Solutions

Qualified engineers, technicians and specialists for short-term peaks, projects or long-term support.

  • Licensed engineers and technicians
  • CAMO and quality staff
  • Short-term and project support
  • Long-term placements
07

Technical Consulting & Project Management

Hands-on experience for your projects, from first planning to handover, including fleet and entry-into-service projects.

  • Fleet and entry-into-service projects
  • Organisation set-up and approvals
  • Project management from planning to handover
  • Independent technical advice
